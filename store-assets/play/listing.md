## Short description (75)

## Full description

VolunTrack is the free, simple way for students to track volunteer and community service hours — for NHS, Key Club, graduation requirements, scholarships and college applications.

LOG HOURS IN A MINUTE
• Add the date, hours, activity and organization, with an optional photo or supervisor signature
• "Log again" fills in a regular activity in one tap
• Works offline and syncs when you're back online

SEE YOUR GOAL
• Set an hour goal and see how close you are, and how much you need each week to finish on time
• A weekly chart and a 🔥 streak for every week in a row you volunteer
• Badges as you go: first hour, 10, 50 and 100 hours, streaks, and more

FIND OPPORTUNITIES NEAR YOU
• Browse volunteer tasks posted by schools and organizations as a list or on a map
• Filter by cause, date, online, or wheelchair accessible
• Sign up in one tap, add it to your calendar, and check in with a QR code when you arrive

REPORTS WHEN THEY'RE DUE
• Download a report of your hours, or share a verified link
• A shareable picture of your hours for a story or an application
• Year in review: your volunteering year at a glance

FOR SCHOOLS AND CLUBS
• Advisers approve hours in one place, set requirements, and download reports
• A starter kit with a join link, QR code flyer and ready-to-send messages
• Move every student up a grade at the start of the year, with an undo

FOR FAMILIES
• Parents can follow their child's hours with their own account
• Students under 13 need a parent's approval to sign up

BRING YOUR FRIENDS
• Share your invite link and earn badges when friends join

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Free for students, volunteers and parents
• We never sell your data
• Available in English and Spanish
